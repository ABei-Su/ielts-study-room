# Changelog

## 0.2.0 - Unreleased

- Warm paper palette, compact subject cards, clearer writing area and resume-first overview. Persistence and authentication unchanged.

- Independent ordinary-password login and server SQLite persistence; Sites data/auth remain intact.
- Refined independent workspace with dark navigation, white subject cards, compact headings and mobile menu dismissal.
- Docker deployment recipe; actual independent hosting pending account/server availability.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-09-28

- Personal Academic IELTS workspace with 15 official sample activities.
- Original audio links, official PDF viewer, answer grading and writing drafts.
- Owner-scoped cloud history, serial autosave, export and local recovery.
- Vocabulary collection, 24 original learning cards and deterministic spaced review.
- Purchased textbook inventory with explicit pending-import status and cloud-saved vocabulary source fields. Book content requires user-supplied pages; order images are not uploaded to the site.

### 学习流程改进
- 练习目录改为紧凑列表，支持全部、继续学习、尚未开始和已完成筛选。
- 首页和目录按最近保存时间继续草稿，保留云端记录格式及自动保存逻辑。
- 听力、阅读增加可键盘操作的题号导航与填写/核对状态。
- 交互参考：github.com/aimerfeng/ists 和 github.com/lincohlesteban-art/ielts-academic-platform；未导入第三方题库或代码。
